owóorí / the tokenizer tax

What does Yoruba pay?

The same 1,012 sentences, professionally translated into 204 languages, priced by 12 tokenizers. English pays 1.00. Everyone else pays a premium: more tokens for the same meaning, which means a higher API bill, more latency, and less room in every context window. Pick a language and read its receipt.

The shape of the tax
Across all 204 languages, 61% pay more than double English's rate at the frontier, and 17% pay more than four times.
Writing Yoruba with its tone and dot-below marks costs +52% under GPT-4o versus the same text stripped bare: a second tax, on the orthography itself.
